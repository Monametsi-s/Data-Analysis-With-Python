### Descriptive Statistics and Data Summarization
- Use pandas 'describe' to get mean, standard deviation, quartiles for numerical data.
- Use 'value_counts' to summarize categorical data frequencies.
#### Data Visualization Techniques
- Box plots visualize distribution, median, quartiles, and outliers for numerical data.
- Scatter plots explore relationships between continuous variables.
#### Exploring Relationships and Correlations
- Use pandas 'groupby' to analyze relationships between categorical variables.
- Pivot tables and heat maps enhance visualization of data relationships.
#### Correlation Analysis
- Correlation measures association between variables; scatter plots with regression lines help visualize this.
- Seaborn's 'regplot' is useful for visualizing correlations.
#### Pearson Correlation Coefficient and P-value
- Coefficient indicates strength and direction of correlation (close to ±1 is strong, near 0 is weak).
- P-value assesses certainty; values < 0.001 indicate strong confidence in correlation.
#### Heatmaps for Correlation Summary
- Heatmaps provide a visual summary of correlation strength and direction among multiple variables.
